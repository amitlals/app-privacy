1.Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the app:

  • Account information — the email address and display name you provide.
  • Learning data — your progress, completed lessons, quiz results, streaks, XP, certificates, and preferences.
  • Code you submit — Python you run in the in-app playground.
  • AI tutor conversations — messages you send to the PyMasterAI tutor.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription (via our payment processor).
  • Basic device & usage information — used to operate and improve the app.
We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize your learning experience.
  • Track your progress, streaks, and achievements.
  • Power the PyMasterAI tutor and code feedback.
  • Process and manage your subscription.
  • Send you notifications you have opted into (e.g., study reminders, hackathon updates).
  • Respond to your support requests and reports.

3.The AI Tutor

When you use the PyMasterAI tutor, the messages you send are processed by our server, which forwards them to our AI model provider (Groq) to generate a response. AI responses are passed through automated safety moderation, and you can report any response you find inappropriate using the in-app "Report" button. We review reports and act on them.

4.Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We use trusted third-party providers (see below) and apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

5.Third-Party Services

PyMaster relies on the following providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

Provider Purpose
Supabase Authentication, database, and cloud sync
Groq AI language model that powers the tutor
RevenueCat Subscription management
Apple App Store App distribution and in-app purchases

6.Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data while your account is active.

Delete your account anytime, in-app. Open Profile → Delete Account to permanently delete your account and all associated data. This removes your data from our servers and your device and cannot be undone. You may also contact us to request deletion.

7.Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and data (available in-app, see above).
  • Export your learning progress.
  • Opt out of marketing or notification communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8.Children's Privacy

PyMaster is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9.Tracking & Selling of Data

PyMaster does not use third-party tracking, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell your personal data. The email and name linked to your account are used solely to operate the app's functionality.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here and revise the "Last updated" date above.
