1.About PyMaster.AI

PyMaster.AI was created with a simple mission: help beginners learn Python in a clear, structured, and engaging way — directly from their iPhone.

The app focuses on making Python learning easier through step-by-step lessons, beginner-friendly examples, practical exercises, and a learning experience designed for users who are just starting their coding journey.

2.Aaradhya's Contribution

The original idea behind PyMaster.AI was inspired by Aaradhya Lal's vision for a simpler and more accessible way to learn Python on mobile devices.

Her learner-first perspective helped shape the direction of the app — including its focus on clarity, simplicity, practical learning, and beginner accessibility.

Aaradhya's contribution reflects the importance of student-driven ideas in technology: identifying a real learning challenge, thinking about how software can solve it, and helping shape a product experience that supports future learners.

5.Product Vision

PyMaster.AI is built around the belief that coding education should be simple, practical, and available to more learners. By combining mobile accessibility with beginner-friendly Python instruction, the app aims to support students, first-time programmers, and self-learners as they begin their programming journey.
